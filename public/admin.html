Tips for a relaxing site

  • Mark your 3–5 favorite photos as "featured" — they rotate slowly behind the hero text on the homepage.
  • Upload .mp3 or .wav files (up to 60 MB each). WAV files get their duration read automatically.
  • Keep photo titles short and calm — they appear over the image on hover.
  • Drag items to reorder them. Order = the order visitors see on the site.
